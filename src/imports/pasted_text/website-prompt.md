MASTER WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

Create a complete, premium, modern, professional, high-converting website for a healthcare support company called “Virtual Assistant Medical.”

The company provides trained Medical Virtual Assistant services to doctors, clinics, physicians, medical practices, healthcare organizations, specialists, and other healthcare professionals.

The website must look like a premium healthcare technology company, not a generic virtual assistant website. It should communicate trust, professionalism, efficiency, reliability, healthcare expertise, security, and modern remote support.

BRAND & VISUAL DIRECTION

Use the existing Virtual Assistant Medical logo prominently in the website header and footer.

Use a strict professional two-color brand palette:

Primary Navy Blue: approximately #063B73
Secondary Teal: approximately #08AAA8

Supporting background colors may use white and very light neutral/off-white shades for readability, but all major brand elements, buttons, icons, headings, illustrations, and accents should remain based on navy blue and teal.

Use modern healthcare-style typography such as Inter, Manrope, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, or similar.

The overall visual style should be:

Premium, clean, modern, medical, corporate, trustworthy, minimal, spacious, polished, technology-focused, and visually impressive.

Avoid cheap-looking templates, excessive gradients, cartoonish illustrations, overly rounded childish designs, cluttered layouts, and generic call-center imagery.

Use high-quality healthcare graphics showing professional remote medical support, doctors working with virtual assistants, medical dashboards, appointment scheduling, secure communication, patient coordination, healthcare administration, and modern digital healthcare workflows.

Create custom-looking icons and graphics using the navy + teal brand identity.

Include subtle modern animations, hover effects, animated counters, scrolling transitions, icon animations, interactive service cards, and clean section reveals without making the website distracting.

Write the website copy in natural, professional, persuasive American English.

Do not use robotic AI-style wording.

Focus messaging on measurable business benefits such as administrative efficiency, reduced workload, improved organization, scalability, communication, and workflow support.

Do not claim that virtual assistants diagnose patients, provide medical advice, approve prescriptions, independently perform clinical decision-making, or replace licensed medical professionals.
